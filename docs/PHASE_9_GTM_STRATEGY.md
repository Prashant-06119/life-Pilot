# Phase 9 — Launch & Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy: Life Pilot

## 1. Objective
Acquire and activate the **first 100 college student beta users** within 90 days of Play Store / Closed Testing launch, validating weekly execution retention without paid ad burn.

---

## 2. Target Segment & Beachhead Focus
- **Initial Beachhead:** 2nd–4th year undergraduate engineering, CS, and commerce/BBA students in Indian & global university campuses actively preparing for internships, campus placements, or technical certifications.
- **Trigger Event:** Start of a new semester, internship prep season (Aug–Oct, Jan–Mar), or mid-semester panic after falling behind on DSA/portfolio/exam goals.

---

## 3. Core Positioning & Messaging
- **Brand Line:** *Life Pilot — Turn career goals into daily execution.*
- **Hero Hook (Pain -> Relief):** *"College classes change, midterms happen, and static to-do lists fall apart. Life Pilot turns your career goal into a real timetable—and recovers missed days in one tap."*
- **Three Value Pillars for Copy:**
  1. **Goal-to-Day Clarity:** AI turns vague career goals ("Backend Intern in 8 weeks") into concrete daily study blocks.
  2. **Schedule-Aware Execution:** Built around fixed college lectures and labs—never schedules impossible 10-hour study days.
  3. **Guilt-Free Recovery:** Missed yesterday? One tap redistributes slipped tasks into open gaps over the next week.

---

## 4. Two-Channel Acquisition Plan (Zero / Low Budget)

### Channel 1: Campus Ambassador & Club Seeding (Target: 60 users)
- **Tactic:** Partner with 5 student coding clubs, placement cells, and developer student communities across 3 colleges.
- **Offer:** Free early-access "Cohort Flight Plan" workshop (20-minute live setup where students import their semester class timetable and generate their 4-week internship roadmap).
- **Referral Loop:** *"Study Wingman"* invite prompt after completing 3 focus sessions—share your weekly roadmap template summary with a classmate preparing for the same role.

### Channel 2: Short-Form Organic Content — Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts (Target: 40 users)
- **Cadence:** 3 short videos/week (30–45 sec) demonstrating real screens:
  1. *"POV: You skipped 3 days of DSA prep before midterms—watch 1-tap Recovery fix my week."*
  2. *"Stop using 4 apps for college: Classes + Career Roadmap + Pomodoro in 1 screen."*
  3. *"How I fit 2 hours of internship prep around an 8 AM–4 PM college timetable."*
- **CTA:** Link in bio / pinned comment -> Play Store Closed/Open Testing track or waitlist form.

---

## 5. 90-Day Milestone Rollout
| Window | Focus | Target KPI |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 1–30** | Closed Beta (20–30 students from 1 campus) | Fix crashes, validate onboarding completion (>= 80%), collect 10 qualitative recovery-flow interviews |
| **Days 31–60** | Multi-Campus Expansion (60 total users) | 3 campus club demos + 12 short-form reels; track Day-7 retention |
| **Days 61–90** | Open Beta & Play Store Launch (100+ active students) | Validate >= 30% WAU/MAU and >= 40% recovery adoption among students with missed tasks |

---

## 6. Funnel & Attribution Tracking Plan
- Track lightweight privacy-respecting milestones:
  1. Google Sign-In completed -> `onboardingCompleted == true` (Activation).
  2. First Goal or AI Plan applied + >= 1 Timetable block added (Setup Complete).
  3. First Focus Session logged or First Task completed (Core Value Realized — "Aha Moment").
  4. First Recovery Plan applied after a missed task (Retention Habit Lock-In).
