\# PHASE 1 — IDEATION



\*\*Objective:\*\* Finalize the painful problem, customer and competitive positioning.



\### Actions

1\. Study the complaints of college students preparing for placements, internships and careers.

2\. Research competing products: MyStudyLife, Forest, StudySmarter, TickTick and Shovel.

3\. Read negative reviews, Reddit discussions and student community conversations.

\### Life Pilot positioning



For college students preparing for careers who struggle to turn their ambitions into consistent daily execution, Life Pilot is an adaptive student career execution platform that connects career goals, academic planning, focused work, learning progress and intelligent task recovery.



Unlike disconnected productivity tools, Life Pilot connects the entire journey from intention to measurable progress.

\# PHASE 6 — AI PROMPTING AND VIBE CODING



\*\*Objective:\*\* Control the coding agent and prevent uncontrolled code generation.



\### Golden prompt structure



Every development prompt must contain:



1\. Context — what already exists.

2\. Goal — one feature to implement.

3\. Requirements — exact behaviour.

4\. Constraints — what must not change.

5\. Acceptance criteria — how to test.

6\. Instructions — plan first, implement after approval.
